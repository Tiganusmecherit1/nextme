# Welcome to the NextMe School

Most fitness apps tell you **what** to do: "eat this", "do 3 sets of 12". Here we want you to understand **why**. When you know what happens in a muscle when you lift a weight, in your blood after a plate of pasta, or in your gut after a serving of sauerkraut, you make better decisions on your own, without depending on memorised rules.

## How the module is built

There are 60 short lessons, grouped into chapters. Each lesson takes 3–6 minutes to read:

1. **Cells and energy** – what we are made of and where energy comes from.
2. **Macronutrients** – protein, carbohydrates, fats, fibre, alcohol.
3. **Micronutrients** – vitamins and minerals, with a focus on what people in Romania often lack.
4. **Water** – water inside cells, hydration and the scale that "jumps".
5. **Body fat** – types of fat and why you cannot lose fat from the spot you choose.
6. **Blood sugar and insulin** – how they work and how to flatten sugar spikes.
7. **The microbiome** – the bacteria in your gut and what feeds them.
8. **Hunger and GLP-1** – the hormones that decide when you feel full.
9. **Muscles** – what happens when you lift.
10. **Bones and tendons** – why weights strengthen your skeleton.
11. **Sleep, stress and body signals**.
12. **Myths busted**.

## How to read a lesson

- **"In practice"** boxes tell you what you can do starting today.
- **"Did you know"** boxes are curiosities that help you remember.
- **"Myth vs reality"** boxes correct things you often hear at the gym or online.
- At the end you get **"In short"**, a summary in a few lines, and the list of **sources**.

Everything is based on official guidelines (World Health Organization – WHO, European Food Safety Authority – EFSA, American College of Sports Medicine – ACSM) and on studies published in scientific journals. When the science is not settled, we say so directly.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Medical disclaimer.** The information in NextMe is educational and does not replace a consultation with a doctor, dietitian or physiotherapist. If you have a chronic condition (diabetes, heart, kidney or thyroid disease), are pregnant, take medication or have an injury, talk to your doctor before changing your diet or training.

## In short

- This module explains **why**, not just **what**.
- 60 short lessons in everyday language, with real sources.
- It is not medical advice: for health problems, see a doctor.
