# How much protein you need (by age)

There is no universal number. Your needs depend on **your weight**, your **age** and **your goal**. That is why we calculate them in grams per kilogram of body weight.

## Quick guide

| Situation | Grams of protein / kg body weight / day |
|---|---|
| Sedentary adult (EFSA minimum) | 0.83 |
| Over 65, healthy | 1.0–1.2 |
| Over 65, active or recovering | 1.2–1.5 |
| Strength training, building muscle | 1.6 (up to ~2.2) |
| Losing weight, keeping muscle | 1.6–2.2 |

Example: a 65 kg woman who does strength training 3 times a week needs about 65 × 1.6 = **104 g of protein per day**, that is 25–35 g at each of her 3–4 meals.

## Why more for exercise

A large 2018 analysis (Morton et al., 49 studies) showed that in people doing strength training, muscle gains rise with protein up to about **1.6 g/kg/day**. Above that value the extra benefit becomes small, although some people may need up to 2.2 g/kg.

## Why more after 60

The muscles of older adults become "deafer" to the protein signal (a phenomenon called **anabolic resistance**). In practice, at 70 you need a bigger protein serving at a meal to get the same building effect as at 25. The PROT-AGE expert group recommends **1.0–1.2 g/kg** for healthy older adults and more for active ones.

This matters a lot, because after 50, muscle loss (sarcopenia) speeds up. Protein **plus** strength training is the best protection (see the lesson on muscle and age).

> [!WARNING]
> **Caution:** if you have chronic kidney disease, your protein needs may be different. Ask your doctor before increasing your intake.

## By age, in short

- **20–40 years:** 0.83 g/kg minimum; 1.6 g/kg if you do strength training.
- **40–60 years:** the same numbers, but reaching them becomes even more important so you do not lose muscle.
- **60+ years:** at least 1.0–1.2 g/kg, ideally ~25–40 g at each main meal.

> [!TIP]
> **In practice:** the most common mistake is a breakfast with almost no protein (coffee and a pretzel). Add 2 eggs, a yoghurt or cottage cheese and you have already covered a quarter of your needs.

## In short

- Adult minimum: 0.83 g/kg/day.
- For muscle: ~1.6 g/kg/day; over 65: 1.0–1.2 g/kg or more.
- Spread protein over 3–4 meals, 20–40 g each.
