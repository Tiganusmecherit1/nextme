# Alcohol: the fourth "macro" you don't need

Alcohol (ethanol) has **7 kcal per gram**, nearly as much as fat. But unlike protein, carbohydrates and fat, the body **does not need** it at all.

## What the body does with alcohol

The body treats alcohol as a toxin. The liver first converts it into **acetaldehyde** (a toxic substance linked to cancer), then into acetate, which it burns for energy.

While it is doing this, it **pauses fat burning**. Alcohol itself is not turned directly into much fat, but the fat in the food alongside it (grilled sausages, fries, peanuts) is more likely to be stored.

## How calorie-dense drinks are

| Drink | kcal (approx.) |
|---|---|
| 1 beer 500 ml (5%) | 210–230 |
| 1 glass of wine 150 ml | 120–130 |
| 50 ml plum brandy (țuică / pălincă, 40–50%) | 115–140 |
| 1 sweet cocktail | 200–300+ |

Three beers in an evening is almost a whole meal, with no useful nutrients.

## Alcohol and training

- **Muscle recovery:** one study (Parr, 2014) showed that alcohol drunk after training reduced muscle protein building by about 24–37%, even when protein was consumed.
- **Sleep:** it makes you fall asleep faster, but disrupts deep and REM sleep in the second half of the night. Poor sleep means poor recovery and more hunger the next day.
- **Hydration:** alcohol increases urine output.

## Alcohol and health

Alcohol is classified by the International Agency for Research on Cancer (IARC) as a **group 1 carcinogen**, the same category as tobacco. In 2023, WHO Europe stated that **there is no safe level** of drinking for health. The idea that "a glass of wine a day is good for the heart" comes from older studies with methodological problems.

> [!CAUTION]
> If you often drink more than you intended or feel you cannot stop, talk to your GP. It is a common and treatable medical problem.

> [!TIP]
> **In practice:** you do not have to be perfect, but be aware. If you drink, drink less often and less, alternate with water, and do not drink on the evening after a hard training session.

## In short

- Alcohol has 7 kcal/g and zero useful nutrients.
- It temporarily halts fat burning and reduces muscle recovery.
- WHO: there is no completely safe level of drinking.
