# Vitamin B12 and folate: blood and nerves

Vitamin **B12** and **folate** (vitamin B9) work as a team. Together they help:

- produce healthy **red blood cells**,
- copy **DNA** when cells divide,
- keep the **nervous system** working.

When one of them is missing, red blood cells come out large and few (macrocytic anaemia), and the person feels tired and short of breath.

## Vitamin B12

**Where you find it:** almost only in **animal foods**: meat, fish, eggs, milk, cheese. Plants do not naturally contain it (except fortified products).

**Who is at risk:**

- **vegans** and strict vegetarians (practically all need a supplement or fortified foods),
- **older adults** (the stomach produces less acid, and B12 is absorbed less well),
- people who take **metformin** (for diabetes) or **acid-reducing medication** for a long time,
- people who have had stomach surgery.

**Possible signs of deficiency:** fatigue, tingling in the hands and feet, memory problems, a red and sore tongue. Prolonged deficiency can damage nerves **permanently**, so it matters to catch it early.

> [!NOTE]
> **Did you know** the liver can store B12 for several years? That is why, after someone switches to a vegan diet, symptoms may appear only after 2–5 years.

## Folate

**Where you find it:** leafy green vegetables (spinach, lettuce, nettles, wild garlic), beans, lentils, chickpeas, broccoli, asparagus, liver, citrus fruit. The name comes from "folium", meaning leaf.

**Why it matters so much before pregnancy:** folate helps the baby's nervous system form correctly in the first weeks of pregnancy, often before the woman knows she is pregnant. WHO recommends **400 µg of folic acid per day** for women trying to conceive, starting before conception, to reduce the risk of neural tube defects.

> [!CAUTION]
> If you are vegan, take metformin or are over 60, ask your doctor for a B12 test. If you are planning a pregnancy, talk to your doctor about folic acid.

> [!TIP]
> **In practice:** one serving of pulses and a handful of greens a day covers much of your folate. For B12, if you eat eggs, dairy or fish a few times a week, you are usually covered.

## In short

- B12 and folate make red blood cells and protect nerves.
- B12 comes from animal foods; vegans need a supplement.
- Folate comes from leafy greens and pulses; essential before pregnancy.
