# How much water you need and how NextMe calculates it

## The official recommendation

For adults in Europe, EFSA considers adequate a **total** water intake of:

- **2.5 litres a day for men**,
- **2.0 litres a day for women**,
- +300 ml in pregnancy, +600–700 ml while breastfeeding.

Important: "total" means **from drinks and from food**. About **20–30%** of water comes from foods (soups, stews, vegetables, fruit, yoghurt). The rest, roughly 1.6–2 litres, comes from what you drink.

## What increases your needs

- **Higher body weight.**
- **Exercise**: an hour of training can add 0.5–1.5 litres.
- **Heat and humidity**: in Romanian summers it easily reaches 35°C.
- **Fever, vomiting, diarrhoea.**
- **Dry air** (radiators in winter, flights).

## How NextMe calculates it

When you sign up, NextMe starts from the EFSA values and adjusts them for **your weight** and **activity level**, giving a daily target between **2 and 4 litres**. On training days the target can go up.

The app then sends you **reminders at fixed times, between 9:00 and 18:00**, until you reach your target. The idea is to drink steadily through the day, not a litre at once in the evening.

> [!NOTE]
> **Did you know** coffee and tea count? Studies show that in regular drinkers, moderate coffee does not dehydrate you: it hydrates almost as well as water. Sugary drinks and alcohol, however, are not a good choice for hydration.

## What to drink

| Good for every day | Occasionally | Not for hydration |
|---|---|---|
| tap or still water | diluted fruit juice | sugary fizzy drinks |
| mineral water | sports drinks (for long efforts) | energy drinks |
| unsweetened tea, coffee | milk, kefir, soups | alcohol |

> [!WARNING]
> **Caution:** the target in the app is for healthy adults. If you have heart or kidney disease or your doctor has set a fluid limit, set your target based on their advice.

> [!TIP]
> **In practice:** keep a 0.5–1 L bottle in sight and drink a glass at each reminder. A glass of water before each meal also helps you feel full.

## In short

- EFSA: 2 L/day for women, 2.5 L/day for men, from drinks and food.
- Needs rise with body weight, exercise and heat.
- NextMe calculates your target (2–4 L) and reminds you at fixed times between 9 and 18.
