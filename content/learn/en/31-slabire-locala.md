# Why you can't lose fat from the spot you choose

Everyone wants to lose fat **from a particular place**: the belly, the "love handles", the thighs, the arms. And many people do hundreds of crunches believing they will melt the fat on their abdomen. Unfortunately, the body does not work that way.

## What the studies say

- **Crunches do not reduce belly fat.** In one study (Vispute, 2011), people who did abdominal exercises 5 days a week for 6 weeks **did not lose more abdominal fat** than the group that did not. Their abdominal muscles got stronger, but the fat on top stayed.
- **Even the opposite.** In another study (Ramírez-Campillo, 2013), participants trained **only one leg** for 12 weeks. They lost fat, but **mostly from the upper body**, not from the trained leg.

## Why: how fat leaves the cell

When you need energy, fat leaves the adipocytes into the **blood**, and the blood carries it wherever it is needed (to working muscles, the heart, the liver). A working muscle **does not take fat from its neighbour** but from the blood, which collects it from the whole body.

And which adipocytes release fat first is decided by:

**1. The receptors on the cells.** Adipocytes have two main types of "buttons" for adrenaline:

- **beta receptors** – "open" the cell and release fat,
- **alpha-2 receptors** – "close" the cell and brake the release.

Areas with many alpha-2 receptors (in many women: hips, thighs, buttocks; in many men: the lower abdomen and "love handles") release fat **more slowly**. That is why they are the last to go.

**2. Blood flow.** Some areas have less blood to carry away the released fat. "Stubborn" fat often also feels colder to the touch.

**3. Hormones.** Oestrogen favours storage on the hips and thighs. Low testosterone and high cortisol favour the abdomen.

**4. Genetics.** Your pattern (apple or pear) is largely inherited. So is the order in which you lose fat.

## The usual order

Typically, fat leaves **first** from the face, arms, chest and visceral fat, and **last** from the "stubborn" areas. That is the hidden good news: even if the belly below the navel does not look different, **the dangerous fat around the organs** often goes down among the first.

> [!WARNING]
> **Myth vs reality:** "slimming creams, heating belts and wrapping your belly in cling film melt fat". They make you sweat locally, so you lose **water**, which comes back with the first glass. They do not burn fat.

> [!NOTE]
> **Did you know** there is only one real way to remove fat from a specific place? Liposuction. And even that does not stop fat growing in other areas if the calorie surplus continues.

> [!TIP]
> **In practice:** train your whole body, keep a moderate calorie deficit and **be patient with the stubborn areas**. They leave last, but they do leave. Abdominal exercises are still useful for strength, posture and your back, just not for fat.

## In short

- You cannot choose where you lose fat; crunches do not burn belly fat.
- Fat is released into the blood and used by the whole body.
- Alpha-2 receptors, blood flow, hormones and genetics decide the order; stubborn areas go last.
