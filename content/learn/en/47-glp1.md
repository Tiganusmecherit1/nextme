# GLP-1: the fullness hormone (and the weight-loss drugs)

You have probably heard of **Ozempic** or **Wegovy**. These drugs mimic a hormone your body already makes: **GLP-1** (glucagon-like peptide-1).

## Where it is made

GLP-1 is made by **L cells** in the gut wall, mostly in the last part of the small intestine and in the colon. It is released when food (especially **protein, fat and carbohydrates**) reaches the gut, and when bacteria ferment fibre.

## What it does

1. **Stimulates the pancreas to release insulin**, but only when blood sugar is raised. It is a "smart" hormone that helps without causing hypoglycaemia on its own.
2. **Reduces glucagon**, the hormone that raises blood sugar.
3. **Slows stomach emptying.** Food stays in the stomach longer, so you feel full for longer and blood sugar rises more gently.
4. **Tells the brain you are full**, acting on the hunger centres in the hypothalamus and brainstem.

## The catch: natural GLP-1 lives only a few minutes

The GLP-1 your body makes is destroyed very quickly by an enzyme called **DPP-4**. **Its half-life is only ~1–2 minutes.** In practice, it does its job locally and disappears.

## GLP-1 drugs

Drugs such as **semaglutide** (Ozempic, Wegovy) or **tirzepatide** (Mounjaro, which also acts on another hormone, GIP) are modified to **last for days** in the blood. They are injected once a week and reach activation levels **much higher and more constant** than natural GLP-1.

In the STEP 1 trial (Wilding, 2021), adults with obesity treated with semaglutide 2.4 mg a week, together with diet and exercise, lost on average **~15% of their body weight** over 68 weeks, compared with ~2.4% in the placebo group.

> [!CAUTION]
> GLP-1 drugs should be taken **only on the recommendation and under the supervision of a doctor**. They have side effects (nausea, vomiting, constipation, rarely pancreatitis and gallbladder problems), contraindications, and can lead to **muscle loss** if not combined with enough protein and strength training. Never buy such products online or from unauthorised sources.

> [!NOTE]
> **Did you know** GLP-1 was first studied for type 2 diabetes? Its strong effect on weight was noticed later and led to drugs dedicated to obesity.

## What this means for you

Even if you do not take medication, **you can support your natural GLP-1** through what you eat and how you eat. The effect is **much smaller** than that of the drugs, but it is real and comes with other benefits. That is what the next lesson is about.

## In short

- GLP-1 is made by the gut after a meal; it stimulates insulin, slows the stomach and makes you full.
- Natural GLP-1 lasts 1–2 minutes; the drugs last days.
- GLP-1 drugs work, but only with a doctor, with protein and strength training.
